## What is included

- Original summaries of hardware, networking, wireless, cabling, RAID, troubleshooting, mobile devices, virtualization, cloud, printers, safety, and professional development.
- Public learning links grouped by subject instead of by Discord channel.
- A structured inventory of the useful PDFs, books, images, quizzes, and spreadsheets found in the course community.
- Clear labels separating official objectives, third-party instruction, and peer-created practice material.

## What is not included

Discord messages, credentials, personal profiles, resumes, raw exports, paid/copyrighted books, instructor slide images, and peer attachments are not redistributed here. Their subjects are indexed so learners can locate authorized copies through their own course access.
