# Course Schedule Snapshot

This is a historical snapshot of the alternating calendar shared on August 28, 2026. Canvas and instructor announcements are the live source of truth.

## Weekly pattern

- Monday: in person.
- Tuesday: remote.
- Wednesday: in person during A weeks; remote during B weeks.
- Thursday: Professional Development in person while PD is active; later becomes an in-person technical day.
- Friday: remote.

- October 12, 2026: Indigenous Peoples' Day — no school.
- November 9, 2026: end-of-course survey shown on the calendar.
- November 11, 2026: Veterans Day — no school.
