## What can be documented

For each future session, record:

- Date, title, subject category, and approximate duration.
- Learning objectives, concepts covered, commands demonstrated, and corrections made.
- Source channel and an authorized playback URL, including whether sign-in is required.
- Original recap notes, timestamps, transcript, captions, and downloadable handouts when sharing permission is clear.
- Recording owner and permission status; omit participant names from the public index unless they explicitly want attribution.

## Recording and privacy rules

- Get the presenter's and participants' consent before recording a voice or video study room.
- Do not publicly upload a recording that displays paid Udemy questions, private Canvas pages, grades, email addresses, notifications, or other account information.
- Prefer an original recap and timestamped topic outline when the stream cannot be shared.
- If a recording is private to the class, label the access requirement and link to the authorized host instead of copying the file into this repository.
