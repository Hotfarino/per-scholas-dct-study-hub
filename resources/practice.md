## Hands-on cabling

- [Ethernet cable termination photo sequence](images.md#ethernet-cable-termination-practice--september-6-2026) — arrange the pairs to one standard on both ends for a straight-through cable, keep twists as close to the plug as practical, trim evenly, seat the conductors fully, crimp, and test.
- A basic tester should confirm pin order and reveal opens, shorts, reversals, and some split-pair faults. Passing continuity alone does not certify a cable for a category rating; formal certification requires appropriate test equipment.

## Home-lab projects

- [ThinkNAS 6-bay project discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/homelab/comments/1n24p9m/thinknas_6bay_version_available/)
- [ThinkNAS 6-bay enclosure files and assembly information](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1737570-thinknas-6x-hdd-nas-enclosure-for-lenovo-m920q#profileId-1846272)

The community design combines a Lenovo M920q-class Tiny PC with a six-drive enclosure, an optional rear RJ45 keystone, and an NVMe-to-SATA controller. Treat it as an advanced lab: verify exact hardware fit, power-supply capacity and quality, grounding, cooling, drive temperatures, SATA-controller support, data backups, and the model author's current instructions before buying parts. Discord cost estimates were anecdotal and are not used as a budget guarantee here.

## Practice workflow

1. Take an untimed baseline quiz.
2. Record each miss by exam domain, wrong assumption, correct rule, and source.
3. Review only the weak sections.
4. Complete the PBQ tasks without notes.
5. Take the timed/shuffled variant.
6. Revisit questions you guessed correctly; a lucky answer is still a knowledge gap.

Peer-created answer keys can contain mistakes. Resolve disputed answers against the current official CompTIA exam objectives or authoritative vendor documentation.
