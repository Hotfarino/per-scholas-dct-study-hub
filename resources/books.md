# Books and Legal Free Access

Availability checked September 2, 2026. This page links only to publisher, author, or official-project sources. A Discord upload is not treated as permission to redistribute a book publicly.

## Artificial intelligence

### *AI Fairness: Designing Equal Opportunity Algorithms* — Derek Leben

**Full legal edition available.** MIT Press provides the complete open-access web edition, including chapter PDFs, under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0 license.

- [Read the complete open-access edition at MIT Press Direct](https://direct.mit.edu/books/oa-monograph/5967/AI-FairnessDesigning-Equal-Opportunity-Algorithms)
- [Publisher information and open-access notice](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262552363/ai-fairness/)

### *The Early-Career Professional's Guide to Generative AI* — Jonas Bjerg

**No authorized free full edition located.** Springer Nature labels the title as subscription content and offers the eBook for purchase. The publisher page provides the table of contents and limited preview material.

- [Official Springer Nature book page and preview](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/979-8-8688-0456-4)

## Linux

### *Learning Modern Linux* — Michael Hausenblas

**No authorized free full edition located.** The author's companion site is free and includes the chapter outline, curated references, code snippets, and configuration files.

- [Official companion site](https://modern-linux.info/)
- [Companion code and configuration files](https://github.com/mhausenblas/modern-linux)
- [Official O'Reilly book page](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/learning-modern-linux/9781098108939/)

### Free legal alternative: *The Linux Command Line* — William Shotts

The author provides the complete Seventh Internet Edition as a free PDF under a Creative Commons BY-NC-ND license. It covers command-line use, common Linux programs, and shell scripting.

- [Official book page and PDF download](https://linuxcommand.org/tlcl.php)

## Python

### *Python Crash Course*, third edition — Eric Matthes

**No authorized free full edition located.** No Starch Press provides a free Chapter 2 sample. The author's companion site provides the full source-code bundle, setup instructions, cheat sheets, updates, and solutions.

- [Author's official companion site and source-code download](https://ehmatthes.github.io/pcc_3e/)
- [Publisher page and free Chapter 2 sample](https://nostarch.com/python-crash-course-3rd-edition)
- [Free official Python tutorial](https://docs.python.org/3/tutorial/)

## Computer architecture

### *Inside the Machine* — Jon Stokes

**No authorized free full edition located.** No Starch Press provides Chapter 4, the table of contents, preface, and introduction as an official free sample.

- [Official publisher page](https://nostarch.com/node/97)
- [Download the official Chapter 4 sample PDF](https://nostarch.com/download/insidemachine_ch4.pdf)
- [Free Nand2Tetris hardware-to-software course](https://www.nand2tetris.org/)

## Access notes

- Try your public or school library for the paid titles; institutional access may already cover O'Reilly or Springer Nature.
- Do not upload paid book files to this public repository unless the copyright holder provides redistribution permission.
- Prefer the official links above because third-party “free PDF” mirrors may be unauthorized, altered, or unsafe.
