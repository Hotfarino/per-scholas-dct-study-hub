# Core 1 Concept Rescue

[Download the illustrated study guide](core-1-concept-rescue.pdf).

A 50-page visual review of eleven commonly confused CompTIA A+ 220-1201 topics. Includes plain-language definitions, acronym meanings, 11 full-color four-panel comic illustrations with guided picture walkthroughs, comparison tables, 33 original multiple-choice questions with explained answers, 12 distinct direct YouTube lesson links, and a final recall sheet. Distances use feet and inches.

Reviewed September 21, 2026 against public 220-1201 objectives and primary technical references. Independent study material, not official or endorsed CompTIA questions. The comic artwork was generated with the built-in image generator and reviewed against the written explanations. It uses simplified teaching analogies, not engineering drawings or pinout references. Videos are linked to Professor Messer's matching 220-1201 lessons.

Study method: read a diagram, explain it aloud, answer the chapter quiz, then check the reasoning. Watch a linked lesson where the concept remains unclear.
