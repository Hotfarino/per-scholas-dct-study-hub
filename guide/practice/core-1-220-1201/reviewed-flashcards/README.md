## Scope and method

The supplied 389-page 220-1101 practice PDF was reviewed against [CompTIA’s public 220-1201 objectives v3.0](https://assets.ctfassets.net/82ripq7fjls2/1oSdlyujpaX3GrM0rir6Ge/91afb2be72785281e8fb4c0d9a70c6f4/CompTIA-A-220-1201-Exam-Objectives-3.0.pdf) and official technical documentation. CompTIA’s private live exam bank was not accessed. This is independent study material, not official or endorsed exam questions, and does not claim full coverage of every 220-1201 objective.

“Keep” refers to the concept, not verbatim wording. “Rewrite” fixes assumptions, explanations or answer errors. “Merge” groups repeated concepts. Items were removed when outside scope, obsolete as framed, unsupported, or not reliably explainable. Older technologies were retained when still relevant. Simulation principles were rewritten as text questions; incomplete or contradictory simulations were removed. Model-specific references illustrate principles; use the actual device’s service manual when working on hardware.

IDs combine the source’s Exam A–H section and question number. PDF page numbers count from the cover. Nonremoved source items map to a concept card, not necessarily every detail of their old scenarios. The original PDF and downloaded third-party documentation are not redistributed.
