## What you will learn

- **NICs and addressing:** network adapters, drivers, MAC/IP addresses, packets, and frames.
- **Local and routed traffic:** managed/unmanaged switches, VLANs, QoS, routers, and default gateways.
- **Wireless and protection:** access points, SSIDs, SOHO combination devices, and firewalls.
- **Provider equipment:** cable/DSL modems and optical network terminals.
- **Cabling and power:** patch panels, PoE switches/injectors, source versus device wattage, Ethernet naming, and common copper distance limits.
- **Apply the ideas:** follow a packet, choose the right device, and complete three original practice questions.

Legacy hubs, repeaters, bridges, and OSI layers provide supporting context. The study guide does not claim that every supporting detail is a separate exam objective.
