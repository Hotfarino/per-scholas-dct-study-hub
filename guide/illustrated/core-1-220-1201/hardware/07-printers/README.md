## What you will learn

- **Printer types:** laser, inkjet, direct thermal, thermal transfer, and impact.
- **Laser process:** processing, charging, exposing, developing, transferring, fusing, and cleaning.
- **Parts and supplies:** drum, toner, fuser, printhead, ribbon, rollers, separation pad, duplexer, and finisher.
- **Setup:** location, drivers, firmware, USB, Ethernet, Wi-Fi, sharing, queues, settings, and secure release.
- **Scanning:** ADF, flatbed, OCR, PDF, JPEG, email, SMB, and cloud destinations.
- **Maintenance and diagnosis:** cleaning, alignment, kits, jams, faded pages, lines, loose toner, offline devices, and frozen jobs.
- **Practice:** 24 flashcards and a three-question quiz with explained answers.

A symptom is a clue, not proof that one part has failed. Printer speeds, duty cycles, supported media, and service intervals depend on the model. Follow its manual for cleaning, cooling, and safe service.
