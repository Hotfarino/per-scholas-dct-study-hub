## What you will learn

- **Components:** motherboard sizes, CPU architecture and compatibility, cooling, RAM generations and pins, expansion cards, firmware, and hardware security.
- **Storage:** HDD versus SSD, SATA versus NVMe, M.2 form factors, removable media, optical capacity, and RAID 0/1/5/6/10.
- **Power and safety:** ESD, safe handling, PSU voltage, wattage and efficiency, connector compatibility, and modular cables.
- **Displays and cables:** LCD/OLED, panel types, resolution and refresh, USB naming and speeds, video connections, and cable reach.
- **Printers:** setup, drivers, scanning, secure printing, seven laser-printing stages, inkjet/thermal/impact methods, and maintenance.
- **Practice:** compatibility checks, a build mission, diagnostic clues, reference tables, and a three-question quiz with explained answers.
